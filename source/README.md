# The Adoption Deck — source note

All content distilled from the borck.consulting practice. Sources:

- `sites/borck.consulting/public/pre-diagnostic-reflection.pdf` — the AI
  Readiness Reflection: 25 written prompts in five sections (1.1–5.5), the
  closing "What your answers tell you" states, and the voice. The audit cards
  are those prompts, one per card, reshaped so the answer lands in writing.
- `sites/borck.consulting/src/` — the five strategic questions (Upadhyay,
  *Artificial Intelligence for Managers*, 2021; Yao & Koidan, *Applied
  Artificial Intelligence*, 2024) and the method sequence
  (Find / Brief / Check / Keep / Deliver).
- The Conversation, Not Delegation framework as the operating principle.

Voice rules borrowed from the site: terse, declarative, plain Australian
English, short imperative sentences, honest disclaimers. The deck states on
its cover what the Reflection PDF states: this is not a sales document, and
there is no score at the end.
