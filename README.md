# The Adoption Deck

Thirty cards for partners and principals of regulated Australian firms asking
how their firm should adopt AI. The free, printable sibling of the AI Readiness
Diagnostic on borck.consulting — the diagnostic in deck form.

**AI can do the work. Your firm is paid for the judgement.**

## The design

Five sections, one per strategic question, each with five audit cards and one
move card, plus an opener and the read-out:

| # | Section | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| 00 | Write, don't think (opener) | gold |
| 01–06 | Can we do AI? — capability and current state | deep sage |
| 07–12 | Should we do AI? — opportunity and risk | rust |
| 13–17 | How would we do it? — implementation orientation | slate |
| 18–22 | Are we ready? — readiness check | ochre |
| 23–28 | What's the value? — business case framing | plum |
| 29 | What your answers tell you (read-out) | gold |

Two card types:

- **Audit cards** — why it gets dodged, what to write down, the line. One
  question from the Reflection, reshaped so the answer lands in writing.
- **Move cards** — why it goes wrong, the move, the line. The one action that
  turns that section's answers into something the firm keeps.
