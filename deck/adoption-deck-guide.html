Answer for the firm, not for yourself. Three checks per section, pen in hand. Be honest: nobody's marking it. The "not yet"s name where the work is.

Capability and current state.

YESNOT YETFor our three main tools, I can say who uses them, how often, and for what.

03 · HOW?Implementation

Implementation orientation.
YESNOT YETAI training has happened, and something measurably changed afterwards.
YESNOT YETWe have an AI use policy that staff could describe in their own words.
YESNOT YETThe next move has one owner and a date — and we can say why it beats the alternatives.

04 · ARE WE READY?Readiness

Readiness check.
YESNOT YETI know where the firm's important written knowledge lives, and it is organised and searchable.
YESNOT YETI know which systems AI would need to integrate with, and which of them are locked.
YESNOT YETThe team has people-time to absorb a meaningful change in the next six to twelve months.
Not yet? Work section 04 — cards 18–22

05 · THE VALUEBusiness case

Business case framing.
YESNOT YETI can state what changes in concrete terms: time on what, quality on what, risk on what.
YESNOT YETI could price our answer to a client questionnaire about how the firm handles AI.
YESNOT YETThe cost of not acting over the next twelve months is written down, in the firm's own terms.
Not yet? Work section 05 — cards 23–28

Your thinnest section
