What's in it

Five strategic questions. Answered in the firm's own words.

CAN WE · 6

Capability

What the firm actually uses today, who uses it, and whether the use is supported or merely tolerated.

SHOULD WE · 6

Opportunity & risk

Where value is plausible, where risk exceeds it, and the answer you'd give your regulator tomorrow.

HOW · 5

Implementation

What the firm would actually do, in what order — and where previous efforts stalled.

ARE WE READY · 5

Readiness

Where the knowledge lives, what the tools must integrate with, and whether the team has the hours.

THE VALUE · 6

Business case

What the work is worth in the firm's own terms — including the cost of not acting.

How to use it

Write, don't think. One section at a time.

1

Thirty to sixty minutes, pen in hand

Audit cards ask the questions a diagnostic would. Written answers expose gaps that mental ones do not.

2

One section per partners' meeting

Move cards turn each section's answers into something the firm keeps: an owner, a page, a date.

3

Read card 29 last

There is no score. There are four states, and each points at different work — consolidation, literacy, conversation, or the discussion itself.
